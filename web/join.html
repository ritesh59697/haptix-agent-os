01Four steps

From phone to badge.

Step 1 is on your phone. Steps 2 to 4 need a computer, because an Agentic Wallet is driven by an AI agent there. There's no website sign-in.

  1. Claim your Agent ID

    In the Binance App: . Pick a name such as yourname.ai and claim it. It's free, one per verified account, and can't be transferred. Binance's announcement

  2. Add the skills to your AI agent

    Use an agent that supports skills, such as Claude Code. Binance's wallet skill signs in and sends. The Haptix skill builds the transactions.
