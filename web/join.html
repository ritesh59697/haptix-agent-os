Four steps

Step 1 is on your phone. Steps 2 to 4 need a computer, because an Agentic Wallet is driven by an AI agent there. There's no website sign-in.

Claim your Agent ID

In the Binance App: . Pick a name such as yourname.ai and claim it. It's free, one per verified account, and can't be transferred. Binance's announcement

Add the skills to your AI agent

Use an agent that supports skills, such as Claude Code. Binance's wallet skill signs in and sends. The Haptix skill builds the transactions.

          Stay safe
          Haptix will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, API secret or sign-in pairing code. Don't share them with anyone. The starter guard and the badge move no money; you only pay gas. Haptix is experimental and not audited. It isn't made by or affiliated with Binance. Keep only what you can afford to lose in any agent-controlled wallet, and check every preview before you confirm.
