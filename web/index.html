Knowledge Base

Frequently asked questions

  • RIP-7212 specifies a native precompile at address 0x100 for P-256 (secp256r1) signature verification. Instead of spending 350,000+ gas on Solidity curve emulation, the precompile executes in native C++ on Base Sepolia and BNB Chain for under 10,000 gas.
  • The smart account enforces a cumulative 24-hour rolling window spend cap in addition to per-operation thresholds. Once the rolling spend meets the daily cap, the wallet automatically locks down all 1-signature transfers and requires the 2-of-2 hardware quorum.
  • Your account is configured with a multi-key quorum. Signer #1 (e.g. your hardware YubiKey or secondary phone) combined with your social recovery or backup key can execute a self-call to replace Signer #0 with a new hardware device.
  • Agents running on Binance Agent OS connect to Haptix via our TypeScript SDK or standard ERC-4337 UserOperations. Agents execute swaps, rebalancing, or arbitrage across Binance and EVM protocols headlessly using Type 0x01 session keys within on-chain SpendPolicy boundaries.
