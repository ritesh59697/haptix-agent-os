For Agentic Wallets with an Agent ID

Limits your agent can't talk its way past.

Haptix keeps spending limits for your Agentic Wallet in a public contract on BNB Chain, keyed to your .ai name. Lowering a limit takes effect at once. Raising one waits out a delay you choose, so a hijacked prompt can't quietly lift it.

Checking contract on BNB Chain… Contract Verified, open source, no admin keys

Get started in three steps

Haptix runs through your own AI agent, such as Claude Code or another client Binance's skills support. There's no website sign-in. Every transaction goes through Binance's preview and your confirmation.

In the Binance App

Claim your Agent ID (Web3 wallet → Agent ID). Then turn on Developer Mode for your Agentic Wallet (Agentic Wallet → Settings). Keep a little BNB in it for gas.

Add the two skills to your agent

Binance's wallet skill signs in and sends. The Haptix skill builds the limit transactions.

How it works

        
Each guard sets three limits per token. Only Agent ID holders can open a guard, and only the wallet that owns a guard can change it.

        

          
daily
Daily cap
The most your agent may pay out in a day.

          
ceiling
Per-payment ceiling
No single payment above this, ever.

          
passkey-above
Passkey threshold
Once the vault is live, larger payments need your device passkey to co-sign.

        

        

          
Instant
Lowering any limit, cancelling a queued change, or making increases wait longer.

          
Delayed
Raising a limit or changing the passkey. It's queued on-chain, visible, and cancellable until your delay passes.

        

      

    


    

      

        
What's live, what's next

        
Plainly stated, so you know what you're getting today.

        

          
Live
Limits on-chain. Open a guard and lower or raise its limits from your Agentic Wallet, with the delay protecting every increase.

          
Next
The Haptix vault. Payments sent through the vault are checked against your guard. They can go only to other Agent ID holders or recipients you allow, and need a passkey above your threshold.

        

      

    


    

      

        

          Risk warning
          Haptix is experimental and hasn't been externally audited. Limits are only as good as the contract and the values you set. AI agents can make mistakes. Keep only what you can afford to lose in any agent-controlled wallet, and check every preview before you confirm.
